## 3. Apple HealthKit Integration

If you choose to connect Ascend to Apple HealthKit, we may read and/or write health and fitness data with your explicit permission.

### Data We May Access

With your permission, Ascend may read:
- Heart rate data
- Active energy burned
- Workout data
- Flights climbed
- Step count

With your permission, Ascend may write:
- Stair stepper workouts
- Associated heart rate and calorie data

### How We Use HealthKit Data

- To display your real-time heart rate during workouts
- To calculate accurate calorie burn estimates
- To sync your Ascend workouts to Apple Health
- To provide comprehensive fitness statistics

### HealthKit Data Protections

**We will NOT:**
- Use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing purposes
- Sell HealthKit data to third parties
- Share HealthKit data with third parties for their marketing purposes
- Use HealthKit data to build user profiles for advertising
- Store HealthKit data on external servers beyond what is necessary for the Service

**We will:**
- Store HealthKit data securely with encryption
- Transmit HealthKit data using secure connections (TLS/SSL)
- Maintain all HealthKit information in accordance with Apple's development guidelines and App Store Review Guidelines

### Managing HealthKit Access

You can disconnect HealthKit access at any time through:
- Your device's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Ascend
- The Ascend app's Settings → Integrations

## 7. Data Storage and Security

### Storage

- **Local Storage**: Your workout data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure data storage
- **Cloud Storage**: Data is synced to Firebase servers located in the United States
- **Media Storage**: Photos and videos are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage with encryption

### Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Encryption of data at rest
- Secure authentication via Sign in with Apple
- Access controls limiting access to personal data
- Regular security assessments and monitoring

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
