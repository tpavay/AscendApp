## Description

```text
Your stair-stepper work should count.

Ascend turns every session into a race up real-world landmarks.
Climb the Empire State Building, Burj Khalifa, Mt. Everest, and more from the stair stepper you already use.
Push for a faster time, chase the leaderboard, and make every step part of a record that compounds.

RACE REAL LANDMARKS

Pick a landmark and attack its step target.
During a Live Climb, motion tracking from compatible AirPods or Beats counts your steps while past attempts become the field you race.
See your position move in real time, then finish the climb or come back stronger.

CLAIM FIRST ASCENTS

New landmarks open with one permanent prize: the First Ascent.
The first climber to finish claims it forever, even when someone later posts a faster time.
Be ready when the next climb drops.

CLIMB THE LEADERBOARDS

Every landmark has its own rankings.
Compare completion times, cadence, and steps against real climbers.
Reach the podium, defend your place, and keep climbing.

BREAK YOUR OWN RECORDS

Best Efforts turns your history into a personal record book.
Track your most steps, longest climb, strongest pace, and other milestones.
See the work stack up instead of disappearing when the session ends.

RUN GUIDED ROUTINES

Build stair-stepper sessions around structured intervals and changing intensity.
Use live heart rate and effort zones with a compatible Bluetooth chest strap.
Review the session when the work is done.

KEEP YOUR HISTORY TOGETHER

Log stair-stepper workouts in Ascend or import supported workouts from Apple Health.
Track progress over time and share the climbs and records you earned.

Ascend is built for people who take the stair stepper seriously.
It is not a generic activity tracker or a social feed.
It is competition, progress, and another reason to climb.

An auto-renewing subscription is required after onboarding.
Available plans, billing terms, and any eligible trial are shown before purchase.
Subscriptions can be managed through your Apple Account, and eligible purchases can be restored in Ascend.

Apple Health is optional.
Live Climbs and sensor-driven routines require compatible motion-equipped AirPods or Beats.
A Bluetooth heart-rate monitor is optional.
Ascend is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
```
