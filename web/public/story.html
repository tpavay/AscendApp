Why I built Ascend

— Tyler

I've always been passionate about fitness, and the stair-stepper became my go-to workout. But I noticed something — no app was built specifically for stair-stepper athletes.

General fitness apps treated it as an afterthought. There was no way to track floors, compare sessions, or see meaningful trends from my stair workouts.

So I decided to build the app I wished existed.

Ascend is purpose-built for anyone who loves the stair-stepper. Whether you're a beginner or you're crushing 100+ floors a session, this app is designed to help you track, improve, and stay consistent.
